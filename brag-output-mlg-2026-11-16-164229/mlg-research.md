## What makes this recognizable

[Pyrocynical's original 2014 upload, mlg teletubbies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vr9O0uR4-Kw) is an era-appropriate primary reference. Its own description credits sudden sad music, airhorn sad violin, people freaking out, and WOMBO COMBO. It demonstrates that this style uses melodramatic reaction contrasts, not merely a bass-heavy track and glitch typography. [Mom get the camera, uploaded by BrightonsBigDay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLRbZJS5A_E) is an available primary source whose uploader explicitly identifies itself as the original poster and copyright owner; it dates the upload to 2011-06-29. Its ecstatic gaming reaction is the appropriate sound for an absurdly celebrated developer achievement.

The following edit grammar is my proposed application of those references and the inspected source clips, not a historical claim about every montage:

1. Aim, impact, reaction. Put a scope on an actual contract, hit it on a beat, then let a recognizable human reaction interrupt the polished film.
2. Escalate a repeated action. Three built-ins are a triple; six become a ridiculous killfeed. The list supplies the joke rather than serving as background to unrelated memes.
3. Use deliberately crusty inserts. Low-resolution footage, hard rectangular crops, a single snap zoom, and Impact or Arial Black outlined reaction text belong here. Keep the underlying NeoHaskell text legible.
4. Leave a fraction of silence before the biggest payoff. A 100-180 ms audio vacuum makes the crowd, sniper, or horn feel larger. Keep the closing trap pulse and finish with one horn, rather than leaving the entire end card silent.
5. Give each sound one job. Hitmarker confirms, sniper makes the trickshot, triple rewards a count of three, camera celebrates, crowd explodes, horn closes. Do not overlap every voice throughout the film.

## Sound and picture handling

Use the parent agent's downloaded originals and preserve its asset provenance record. Trim the 8.6-second crowd to the actual shout, the roughly 5-second triple clip to its recognizable phrase, and the 3-second camera clip to the shortest complete phrase. Audition the selected intervals. No slurs or hateful material are needed for this style, and none is present in the inspected silent visual selections. Avoid importing full compilation soundtracks whose unrelated speech has not been checked.

Keep reaction voices above the music rather than making the whole mix louder. Proposed starting mix: duck music about 8 dB during camera and triple, 4 dB during crowd; bring it back over 80-150 ms. Let hitmarker transient cuts remain sharp while keeping master peaks controlled. These values are edit recommendations, not claims of a completed mix.

Use hard cuts and 3-6-frame snap zooms for surprise. Avoid constant high-contrast full-frame strobing. The lower-resolution reaction texture already supplies chaos. A readable benefit followed by a 0.4-1.4-second reaction is stronger than covering every frame with every asset.

Render the animated inserts as decoded video or deterministic frame sequences rather than trusting a browser GIF's independent wall clock. Place all meme media below the essential product title when they share a frame. The viewer should still understand NeoHaskell's benefit with the sound muted.

## Product boundaries

The approved pitch is an AI-first language that helps one developer understand and build an entire application, with integrated tools and AI assistance. This is the repository's own intended-product positioning in `PRODUCT.md` and the positioning skill, not a staffing guarantee. Existing copy gives the joke its subject: enforced decisions, preserved contracts, built-ins, executable specification, and recorded history.

Keep `NeoHaskell`, `AI-first language`, and `neohaskell.org` on the final card. Do not replace product proof with fake benchmarks, customer endorsements, a pretend celebrity recommendation, or claims such as zero bugs, unhackable, or AI cannot break it. A visibly borrowed reaction cut is a punchline; do not write dialogue that implies the featured people have evaluated NeoHaskell.

The explicit owner request authorizes an intentionally loud montage parody in this separate film. Unslop's useful constraints here are purposeful effects, product-specific jokes, coherent repetition, and readable content. Generic UI restraint should not flatten the requested parody back into the already-approved cinematic version.
