# Composition assets

NeoHaskell logo and authentic IDE image: copied without alteration from this repository's public assets. Screenshot provenance is maintained in .impeccable/reviews/model-evolution-2026-11-13/source-evidence.md.

Sansation, Manrope, and Pixelify Sans: copied from public/fonts with their bundled OFL licenses.

Music: Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0). Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 12. Source: https://ende.app/en. License: https://ende.app/en/standard-license. The film uses the first 20 seconds with volume adjustment and edge fades.

Sound effects: Kenney, CC0, from the brag skill's bundled interface and impact sets.

GSAP: copied from the installed gsap package. License: https://gsap.com/standard-license/.

The text arrival pattern adapts the HyperFrames registry's per-word-rise component to two fixed lines, preserving its overshoot-free arrival and still hold. The original fetched component is retained under compositions/components/ as source provenance; it is not mounted.

Audio-data extraction was attempted through media-use/audio/scripts/extract-audio-data.py. It was unavailable because the system Python environment lacks numpy. Audio-reactive motion is omitted rather than replaced with fake pulsing. Musical cues are from the bundled track's analysis; the cancellation starts at 13.11 seconds.
