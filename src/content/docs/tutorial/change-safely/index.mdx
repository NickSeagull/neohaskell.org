## Page plan

This page will add one requirement to the tested shop feature and follow its effect through the model, source, and checks. It will show how to give the agent a bounded change, inspect a focused diff, and rerun both new and existing behavior cases. Before-and-after examples will make unintended changes visible. The chapter will finish with a reviewed backend change and link to [Work with an AI agent](/guides/ai-workflow/) for the reusable review and recovery process.
