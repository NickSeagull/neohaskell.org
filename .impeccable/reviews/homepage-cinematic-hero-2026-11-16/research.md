# Adversarial hero direction, 16 November 2026

## Decision

Build one full-width typographic composition above a panoramic macro relief: thick violet planes with a single continuous cyan inset channel, visible folds, and precise material joins. Crop the relief into the hero's lower and right edges so it becomes the composition's physical foundation. The open-workshop maquette premise was rejected during art review because generated images read as architectural pavilions. This refined relief is conceptual brand material, not a model of application execution or a claim about a particular feature. The real IDE immediately below supplies product evidence.

Keep the original small navigation logo, the approved headline "Build with AI. Keep control.", the existing explanatory sentence and two actions. Retain the accepted geometric font and limited pixel emphasis. Retire the giant logo and its ghost outlines completely. Lower sections remain unchanged.

This is a proposed hero direction under the owner's authorization to build. Neither this recommendation nor a prior review verdict establishes owner acceptance.

## Current hero: why the rejection is justified

1. The right half repeats the small navigation logo at monumental scale. That repeats identity but creates no new association with building or human control. Its black outline makes it read as a sticker pasted beside a standard marketing column.
2. The composition consists of two independent objects, headline and logo, with large empty margins. It has scale but no staging, material, or spatial relationship. Making the same mark larger cannot solve that premise.
3. The faint trailing logo outlines read as a convenient CSS effect. They do not express a product relationship and weaken the silhouette.
4. On the phone, the giant logo follows an entirely conventional centered headline, description, and button stack. The entire first viewport is consumed before any product evidence appears.
5. Copy and identity are not the broken part. The approved headline communicates agency and the paragraph establishes the product category. Replacing them with a technical diagram would impose learning before desire, repeating an already rejected approach.

## Build specification

### Five-line direction

Product: an AI-first language for building whole applications while retaining understanding and judgment.

Character: deliberate, capable, inventive.

World: a precision workshop, expressed through material relief and intentional joins rather than a literal building or machine.

Moment: one panoramic violet material relief with a continuous cyan inset path, physically lit and cropped into the page.

Signature: the original logo's angular cuts echoed in the object's folds, without forming another logo.

### Scene and composition

Use two or three thick violet planes with a single purposeful continuous cyan inset channel. Their precise folds and joins echo the original logo's angular cuts without recreating its outline. This is a macro detail, with no complete object silhouette. It must not suggest a building, machine housing, room, roof, stairs, entrance, toy city, motherboard, floating cubes, portal, or central logo sculpture. No labels, arrows, diagrams, text, or fake interface.

Material: satin violet with fine visible surface texture, a pale cyan channel with restrained physical shading, and darker violet inner faces. Directional light describes edges and thickness. No ambient purple fog, added neon bloom, glitter, or abstract galaxy. The generated cyan is already bright enough; do not add CSS lighting effects.

Camera and placement: panoramic close detail, with an open alpha area toward the upper-left and a definite rising diagonal toward the right. Anchor the relief to the lower edge of the hero and extend it beyond the content grid. Do not display it as a separate right-column image or a freestanding card. The cutout should define the calm field available for the headline and paragraph, with no hard object edge crossing readable text.

Compose desktop as one field approximately 680-760px tall under navigation. Headline starts near the upper-left, around 88-104px at 1440px, with purposeful line breaks. Paragraph remains 18-20px and about 430-480px wide. Actions sit directly below it. The scene may reach behind the headline's outer whitespace, but no texture or hard edge should interfere with letters. Show a glimpse of the next actual-product section at common laptop heights.

### Mobile

Use a deliberate portrait crop of the same relief with its cyan turn and material thickness visible. Keep the headline, paragraph, and primary action in the first 500-560px. Place artwork below and partly to the right, about 240-300px high, avoiding a separate long image section. Left alignment is a useful way to break the rejected centered stack. Do not create horizontal scrolling or scale essential text. No essential meaning may depend on hover or motion.

### Themes and movement

The preferred still should work as a material scene in both themes. Light mode can use a pale lilac ground and dark violet type. Dark mode needs a genuinely integrated dark ground or carefully judged crop, not a black fog overlay that hides the object. Preserve saturated cyan and violet surfaces. If one raster cannot integrate convincingly, generate or art-direct a second theme treatment rather than placing a pale rectangle on dark UI.

Motion is optional. A single short opacity/position entrance can establish the scene. No perpetual floating or rotating; no scroll hijacking. Reduced motion must display the completed still. An excellent static composition is a stronger result than an animated generic object.

## Adversarial gates for the implementation

1. Does the relief create a deliberate spatial foundation for the type, or does it read as gaming-laptop wallpaper? Reject a generic decorative background. The path should remain one considered material form, without extra ornamental effects.
2. Do title and object make one composition, or did we recreate the same two-column hero with a different right-side illustration? Reject another detached illustration slot.
3. Does the phone preserve a striking material detail and an immediate docs action? A scaled-down desktop object beneath a full page of copy repeats the original weakness.
4. Does the original logo remain intact and modest, with no new claims, fake UI, concept labels, auxiliary caption, or added teaching burden? The visual is atmosphere; actual product evidence remains below.
5. Do both themes retain crisp readable text and material depth? Inspect raw screenshots at 1440 and 390, then a 1280x800 fold. Technical passing checks alone are not acceptance.

## First art review: subject premise correction

The generated candidate at `/Users/nick/.codex/generated_images/01a1e42a-85b1-7c23-8a86-53921c19753a/exec-12a75991-f253-4e5d-bbe7-2d2ddc01221e.png` is rejected before implementation. Its roof, columns, floor, and stairs unmistakably describe a futuristic pavilion. Violet haze also conflicts with the desired physical lighting. Removing stairs alone would leave the wrong product-category association.

The doubtful assumption is that a complete architectural workshop or machine maquette can express a programming language without suggesting a physical architecture product. The next art pass should refine the subject to a tightly cropped material relief, borrowing the verified Osmo composition: two or three thick, machined violet planes with purposeful interlocking cyan channels and visible precise joints. The structure must have one continuous folded path, not random solids. Crop away any complete building silhouette. No roof, pillars, floors, room, entrance, housing development, or inhabitable scale. This conveys deliberate structure as brand material without claiming that NeoHaskell is a physical object.

The selected composition, typography, palette, immediate actions, and actual IDE evidence remain. The revised subject is an art-direction correction, not a new product claim. The next image must be judged independently; a beautiful image of the wrong subject still fails.
